Frequently asked

Does Rainier replace the existing SIEM or XDR?

No. It layers agentic investigation and response onto existing tools and workflows.

What do Rainier's agents do?

They handle intake, enrichment, correlation, triage, investigation and risk scoring, with observable, replayable handoffs.

Who approves disruptive response actions?

Analysts. Agents assemble evidence and propose actions; people approve steps that could affect production systems.
