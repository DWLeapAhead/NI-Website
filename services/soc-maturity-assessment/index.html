Frequently asked

What does a maturity assessment actually produce?

A scorecard against a recognized SOC maturity model, a gap analysis, and a prioritized roadmap covering people, process, and tooling.

Do you assess SOCs you don't run?

Yes. The assessment is independent of whether Network Intelligence operates your SOC.

How often should we reassess?

Most teams reassess annually, or after a major change in tooling, scope, or a significant incident.
