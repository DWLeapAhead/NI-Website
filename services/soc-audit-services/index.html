Frequently asked

Which SOC report do we need?

SOC 1 covers controls relevant to financial reporting; SOC 2 covers the five trust service criteria; SOC 3 is a public-facing summary of SOC 2 results. We help you scope the right one before the engagement starts.

How long does readiness take?

It depends on current control maturity, but most clients run a readiness assessment first to close gaps before the formal audit window.

Do you also run the SOC that gets audited?

We can, through our managed SOC and SOC-as-a-service offerings, but SOC audit services are available independently.
