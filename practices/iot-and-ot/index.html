Frequently asked

Does this require replacing existing OT systems?

No. It's designed to work with SCADA, PLC, and DCS systems already in place, layering visibility and security on top.

Who typically owns this practice internally?

Usually a mix of operations leaders, plant/maintenance teams, and the security team — uptime and safety sit alongside security here.

What frameworks does the assessment follow?

NIST and C2M2 are the primary reference frameworks, applied to the specific risk profile of the site.
