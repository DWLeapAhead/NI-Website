Best Security Audit Software to Ensure Compliance & Mitigate Risk

Rapid7 InsightVM, Qualys Policy Compliance, Drata, Vanta, LogicGate, and Tugboat Logic compared on what each is actually built to solve.

Richa Arya · Updated 2026

Security audit software spans two related but distinct jobs: finding technical vulnerabilities (scanning) and proving your controls are actually in place and documented (compliance automation). The right pick depends on which problem is more pressing — or whether you need both.

Tools compared

  • Rapid7 InsightVM — Strong vulnerability prioritization with real-time risk scoring and broad asset coverage across cloud, container, and on-prem environments. Trade-off: Pricing and feature depth can be steep for smaller teams primarily after basic compliance checklists.
  • Qualys Policy Compliance — Deep policy and configuration compliance checks mapped to major frameworks, backed by a mature, widely-deployed scanning engine. Trade-off: Interface and reporting can feel dense; best suited to teams with a dedicated compliance function.
  • Drata — Strong automated evidence collection and continuous control monitoring purpose-built for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and similar audits. Trade-off: Lighter on deep technical vulnerability scanning — it's a compliance automation platform first.
  • Vanta — Fast time-to-value with pre-built integrations and a clean audit-readiness dashboard across common frameworks. Trade-off: Similar to Drata, it's focused on compliance evidence rather than technical vulnerability assessment.
  • LogicGate — Flexible, workflow-driven GRC platform that can model custom risk and compliance processes beyond out-of-the-box templates. Trade-off: More configuration overhead upfront compared to templated compliance-automation tools.
  • Tugboat Logic — Guided, checklist-style compliance workflows aimed at teams running their first SOC 2 or ISO audit. Trade-off: Less suited to organizations that already have a mature GRC program and need deeper customization.

How to choose

If the immediate need is passing a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 audit with minimal manual evidence-gathering, Drata, Vanta, and Tugboat Logic are purpose-built for that. If the priority is finding and prioritizing actual technical vulnerabilities across a sprawling environment, Rapid7 InsightVM and Qualys Policy Compliance are the stronger technical fit. Teams with complex, custom risk processes that don't map cleanly to a template often end up at LogicGate instead. Many mature security programs end up running one tool from each category rather than expecting a single platform to do both well.

Frequently asked

Do I need both a vulnerability scanner and a compliance automation tool?

Most mature programs end up running one of each — a scanner like Rapid7 or Qualys for technical findings, and a platform like Drata or Vanta for audit evidence and control monitoring.

Which tool is fastest to get audit-ready with?

Drata and Vanta are generally the fastest for SOC 2 or ISO 27001 readiness thanks to pre-built integrations and automated evidence collection.

Is LogicGate a good fit for a first-time audit?

It can be, but its flexibility means more configuration work upfront — Tugboat Logic's guided workflows are usually a gentler starting point for a first audit.
