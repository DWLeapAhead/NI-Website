Drata vs. Vanta: Full Comparison of Features, Pricing & More

Where the two leading SOC 2 automation platforms actually differ — and which fits a first-time audit versus a complex DevOps environment.

Nupur Swetambari · Updated 2026

Automation and focus

Both are SaaS platforms built around continuous monitoring, automated evidence collection, and compliance dashboards. Drata leans toward deeper, more developer-oriented automation; Vanta leans toward usability and faster onboarding for teams without a dedicated compliance function.

Ease of use

Vanta is generally seen as more intuitive for non-technical users. Drata is powerful but carries a steeper learning curve, which tends to pay off most for teams with the engineering bandwidth to configure it deeply.

Pricing

Vanta is generally cheaper to start — roughly $7,500–$10,000 per year for smaller companies. Drata's foundational plans start higher, around $15,000 per year. Both scale with employee count, framework count, and integrations, and exact pricing needs a quote either way.

Integrations and API

Both offer broad integration ecosystems. Drata's API is generally considered more advanced, which matters for complex or highly custom environments that need to build around the platform rather than just plug into it.

Framework coverage

Both support SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, and PCI DSS. Drata is more often cited for greater customizability and granular policy control.

Support

Both are generally well-regarded on support. Vanta is noted for responsiveness; Drata for setup guidance during initial onboarding.

Which one fits

There's no universal winner. Vanta tends to suit first-time SOC 2 candidates, small-to-mid SaaS teams, and startups that need fast, low-friction readiness. Drata tends to suit tech-savvy teams with complex DevOps environments who want granular control and room to scale. Teams in regulated industries, or without in-house compliance expertise, often do better with a hybrid model — software plus managed advisory — regardless of which platform they pick.

Other names worth a look in the same category: Secureframe, Sprinto, Thoropass, and Hyperproof.
